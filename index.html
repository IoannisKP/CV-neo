How I work

Clarity before polish.

Product work is deciding what deserves to exist, making competing priorities visible, and finding evidence strong enough to change your mind.

Research before certainty

Interviews, synthesis, journey mapping, wireframes, and think aloud testing replace assumptions with something observable.

Comfort with complexity

Thirteen years in a regulated service business taught me to connect requirements, operations, stakeholders, and incomplete information.

Verification over theatre

I use AI tools every day and assume plausible output still needs checking. The audit is part of the product work.
