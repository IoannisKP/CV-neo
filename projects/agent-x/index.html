01 · Overview

Guidance without an easy mode.

Escape from Tarkov asks players to learn through uncertainty. That makes failure meaningful, but it can also leave a newcomer unable to understand what to try next.

This supervised student project followed one complete research cycle. I interviewed players, organised the evidence in a matrix, defined two working personas, mapped the emotional journey, designed a narrow intervention, and evaluated its interaction logic.

Question

Can contextual guidance help a new player continue learning without making the game easier?

Response

A companion that stays quiet until repeated failure suggests that help could be useful.

Outcome

The interaction was understandable. Its value to the target audience still requires a better recruited study.

02 · Challenge

The friction is both the feature and the risk.

The obvious response would be more tutorials or clearer instructions. Research reframed the challenge: preserve difficulty, but help players interpret failure well enough to form another hypothesis.

04 · Research matrix

From interview signal to product decision.

The matrix made the reasoning traceable. Each design response had to connect to a pattern in the interviews, and every row kept an unanswered question visible.

Interview signal Interpretation Product response Still unknown
Failure can motivate when it teaches something. Difficulty needs a legible relationship between action and outcome. Offer context that helps the player form another hypothesis. How much context is enough for a target player?
Help offered too early can interrupt discovery. Timing changes whether guidance feels useful or intrusive. Keep the companion silent until repeated failure indicates need. What trigger should activate the intervention?
Players value making their own decisions. Removing uncertainty would also remove part of the experience. Point toward a direction without revealing a complete route. Where is the boundary between a clue and a solution?
New and returning players carry different knowledge. A single onboarding response would treat unlike needs as equal. Use the Seeker as the primary profile and the Returner as a check. Would the Returner need a different intervention?

The matrix records design interpretations, not claims about the whole player population.

06 · Concept

A companion that knows when to stay quiet.

Agent X presents guidance as part of the game world. It does not begin by explaining everything. It waits for evidence of struggle, then offers enough context for another decision.

Repeated failure+Unclear next step=Offer one clue
Trigger

After demonstrated need

Support responds to struggle instead of assuming it.

Form

Inside the world

The voice and timing protect the tone of the game experience.

Boundary

Point, do not solve

The clue creates direction while the decision remains with the player.

08 · Testing

The interaction read clearly. The sample did not.

Three participants aged 40 to 46 completed think aloud sessions. They found the guidance and understood its purpose without being directed toward it. None had played Tarkov, so the study could not establish value for the Seeker.

Evaluation questionEvidenceConclusion
Can people notice the guidance?All three participants located it without prompting.Initial support
Can people understand its purpose?All three understood what the prompt was doing.Initial support
Does the target audience value the intervention?No participant matched the Seeker profile.Unanswered
Does deliberate silence feel appropriate?The sample lacked the game context needed to judge it.Unanswered
A usable interaction is not the same as a validated product decision.

09 · Reflection

Name the weak evidence, then design the next study.

The project produced a clear concept and a useful early signal. It also exposed a recruitment mistake. The next round should answer a value question with actual target players, not repeat a comprehension test with convenient participants.

01

Recruit against the Seeker

Include new Tarkov players who are motivated to learn but struggling to interpret repeated failure.

02

Compare timing thresholds

Test whether guidance is better after one failure, repeated failure, or an explicit request.

03

Measure renewed agency

Observe whether a clue helps players form a new plan, not merely whether they can see the interface.

Current conclusion

The concept is coherent. The value hypothesis is still open.

Agent X now has a traceable path from interviews to matrix, personas, journey, product rules, prototype, and evaluation. Its next responsible step is a study with the people it was designed to help.
